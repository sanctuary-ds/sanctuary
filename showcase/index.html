I · Flavors

The same building, four hours of light

A flavor is the canonical hour at which you stand in the nave. Stone, ink and shadow change with it; every pigment is re-toned so it stays legible. Each card below is themed on its own, nested inside this page.

II · Pigments

Twelve glasses, eleven tones each

Pigments are flavor-independent ramps generated in OKLCH, so a tone number means the same lightness in every hue. The outlined tone is the one the current flavor uses as that glass. Click any tone to copy its hex.

Stone, lead and ink of this flavor

III · Glazing

One accent, three ways of holding colour

Glazing decides how the accent is rendered on a filled element. It is a separate axis from the accent itself, so any pigment works in any glazing.

IV · Windows

Light that is cast, not applied

The window colours ambient light (the rose above, beams, glows, hero gradients) while the accent keeps doing the precise work. It always follows the accent: alone, or leaded with its two neighbours on the hue wheel.

Named windows

Five fixed gradients for a single place, such as a cover or an illustration. They do not follow the accent and are not a theme switch. Click one to copy its token.

VI · Light

Lumen: elevation is illumination

In a dark building, what is nearer to you is not only shadowed from behind, it is better lit. Each Lumen level pairs a lighter stone with a deeper umbra; at level 5 only the umbra deepens. Cast is the separate, coloured glow that glass throws on its surroundings.
